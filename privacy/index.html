Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

Privacy Messenger is end-to-end encrypted messaging built for people who actually want privacy. This policy explains exactly what we handle.

Summary: Your messages are end-to-end encrypted. We literally cannot read them. We collect the absolute minimum required to deliver messages.

What we collect

  • Email address — used for account login and recovery only
  • Encrypted message blobs — stored in transit until delivered, encrypted with keys we do not have
  • Device push tokens — only if you enable push notifications, used to wake your device when a message arrives
  • Basic anonymous analytics — uptime, error counts, no per-user tracking

What we cannot see

  • Your message content (it is encrypted before leaving your device)
  • Who you message with, in plaintext form (metadata is minimized)
  • Your contacts list
  • Your files or attachments (also end-to-end encrypted)

What we never collect

  • Phone numbers
  • Real names
  • Profile photos sent to our servers (avatars are local)
  • Advertising IDs
  • Cross-app trackers

How messages work

Messages are encrypted on the sending device using keys that never leave your device unencrypted. The encrypted blob travels through our server only as ciphertext, is delivered to the recipient device, and is decrypted there. We have no way to read this traffic — not for advertising, not for analytics, not for law enforcement.

Self-destruct messages

If you enable a timer on a message, the recipient device deletes the message after the timer expires. The server-side copy (which was encrypted anyway) is removed once delivered.

Data deletion

You can delete your account from the app's settings. This removes your email, push token, and any pending encrypted messages from our servers. Messages already delivered to recipients remain on their devices — we have no way to recall them.

Open source

The Privacy Messenger backend is open source so the privacy claims can be verified. We don't ask you to trust us — we ask you to read the code.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS / WSS. Messages use AES-256-GCM with X25519 key exchange. Bug reports and disclosures: security@morvenempire.com

Children

Privacy Messenger is for users 13 and older.

Contact
